About the Project

Model a guitar string as masses connected by springs. Construct a tridiagonal stiffness matrix and calculate eigenvalues and eigenvectors to determine natural frequencies and visualize the first three vibration modes.

Instrument presets below are illustrative mathematical models, not measured instrument data.

Mathematical Formula

Equation of motion: Mx″ + Kx = 0

Eigenvalue equation: Kφ = λMφ

Angular frequency: ω = √λ

Frequency: f = ω / (2π)

For fixed ends, the stiffness matrix has 2k on its main diagonal and −k on the adjacent diagonals. The mass matrix is M = mI.
